Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to all enquiries submitted via soundcorestudio.ng

Soundcore Studio Limited ("Soundcore", "we", "us") is a creative production studio incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria under CAMA 2020 (RC 9191853), with its registered address in Ilorin, Kwara State. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it. It is written to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

The only place this website collects personal data is the booking form on contact.html. When you submit that form, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number or WhatsApp handle, if you choose to share it
  • The service you're enquiring about and the package tier you're considering
  • Whatever you type in the project details field — typically date, genre, references, and what you're trying to achieve

We also receive basic technical metadata from Formspree (the third-party service that powers our form), including the timestamp of your submission and your IP address. We do not place analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels on this website. We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics, or any similar tool.

Why we collect it

We collect this information for a single purpose: to respond to your booking enquiry with availability and a tailored quote. Your name and contact details exist so we can reply to you. Your project details exist so the reply can be specific to what you're trying to make. We do not use your data for marketing, we do not sell it to third parties, and we do not use it to train any model or automated system.

Our legal basis for processing this data under the NDPR is your consent — given at the moment you voluntarily submit the form — combined with our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry you initiated.

Who sees it

Your submission is handled by Formspree (formspree.io), a U.S.-based form-processing service. By submitting the form, you acknowledge that your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers for the purpose of forwarding it to our inbox. Formspree processes data in line with GDPR and SOC 2 Type II standards; their privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Inside Soundcore, only the studio's booking team reads enquiry submissions. We do not share, rent, or sell personal data to any other third party.

How long we keep it

Formspree retains submissions on their servers according to their default retention schedule. On our end, we keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months after the last message in the thread, so we have context if a client returns to book a follow-up session. After that, we delete the email thread.

If you become a paying client, your contact details and project brief may be retained for the duration of our working relationship plus 6 years for accounting and tax-compliance purposes, as required under Nigerian law.

Your rights under the NDPR

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about what data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data ("the right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent to processing at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe we have mishandled your data

To exercise any of these rights, email bookings@soundcorestudio.ng with the subject line "Data request" and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS. The contact form transmits your data over an encrypted connection. Our Content-Security-Policy is strict: no inline scripts, no third-party trackers, no advertising networks. The only third-party services that touch your data are Formspree (form handling) and your own email provider (when we reply).

No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure. We work hard to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's data

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted the booking form, please contact us and we will delete the submission immediately.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and bump the "last updated" date above. We won't make retrospective use of your data in a way that's inconsistent with the policy in effect at the time you submitted it.
