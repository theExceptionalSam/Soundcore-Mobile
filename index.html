About The Brand

Built from the
core, for the world.

Soundcore Studio Limited is a creative production studio rooted in African culture and driven by global standards. We focus on music production, audio engineering, podcast production and content shooting.

We exist to capture, shape and amplify sound and stories from the core blending creativity, technical excellence and emotional depth to produce powerful audio and visual content for artists, creators, brands, churches and media platforms worldwide.

CreativeProfessionalConfident AuthenticCulture-drivenGlobally competitive
Our Mission

To create high-quality sound and visual experiences by combining creativity, technology and professionalism helping creators and brands communicate their stories clearly, powerfully and authentically.

Our Vision

To become a globally respected, African-rooted creative studio known for excellence in audio and visual production, culture-driven storytelling, and world-class content creation.

What We Do

Six disciplines.
One standard.

From the first take to the final export, every service is built around one commitment: it should sound and look better leaving Soundcore than you imagined it could.

01
Music Recording, Mixing & Mastering

Vocals and instrumentation captured clean, balanced into a cohesive mix, and mastered loud, wide and competitive for every streaming platform.

02
Live Session & Performance Recording

Bands, choirs, churches and ensembles recorded in the room with multi-track capture with professional mic placement and on-site technical direction.

03
Podcast Recording, Editing & Production

Clean conversation audio, structured editing and full production built for shows that need to hold attention episode after episode.

04
Content Shooting

Video production and creative visuals for music videos, reels, interviews and brand content with lit, framed and shot with intention.

05
Audio & Visual Post-Production

Colour, sound design, edit and finishing. The layer that turns raw footage and rough audio into a polished, release-ready piece.

06
Horn Recording & Arrangements

Impeccable horn recording and arrangement, intentionally crafted for your genre and delivered mix-ready.
